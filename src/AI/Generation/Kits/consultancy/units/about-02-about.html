Northfold started in 2014 with three people and one conviction: that advice should be judged by what changes after the advisers leave.

Our founders had spent their careers on the receiving end of strategy projects (running depots, underwriting books and programme offices) and had seen too many good recommendations die in the gap between a board paper and a Monday morning. So we built a firm that stays through that gap.

We have grown slowly on purpose. Every consultant here has run something before advising on it, partners lead no more than two engagements at a time, and we remain wholly owned by the people who work here.
