Principles

Five rules we hold ourselves to.

They are written into every proposal, and clients are invited to score us against them at the end.

  1. 01

    Start with the decision

    Analysis is only useful if someone can decide something with it. We name the decision and its owner before we open a spreadsheet.

  2. 02

    Use the client’s own numbers

    Benchmarks start arguments; reconciled transaction data ends them. Our fact bases are signed off by your finance team.

  3. 03

    Say what would change our mind

    Every recommendation ships with its assumptions and the evidence that would overturn it.

  4. 04

    Stay until it moves

    A report is a milestone. We measure ourselves on the metric we agreed in week one, and part of our fee depends on it.

  5. 05

    Leave people stronger

    Every engagement ends with named client owners who can run what we built without us.
