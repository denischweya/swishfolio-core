How we work

From open question to moved number in four steps.

Every engagement follows the same spine, whether it lasts six weeks or eighteen months. You always know which step you are in and what you will have at the end of it.

Milestones hit on time

Across all delivery engagements, 2023–2026

Our engagement models
01Week 0–1

Frame

We agree the decision you actually need to make, who makes it and by when. If the question is wrong, we say so before you have paid for an answer.

  • One-page decision brief
  • Named decision owner
  • Success metric and baseline
02Weeks 2–5

Diagnose

We work from your own transaction-level data, reconciled to the ledger, and from time spent where the work happens: depots, wards, underwriting floors.

  • Fact base your finance team signs
  • Root causes ranked by value
  • Interviews across every level
03Week 6

Decide

Three real options, each costed, each with its risks named. We recommend one and tell you what would change our mind.

  • Options paper for the board
  • Business case with ranges
  • A written recommendation
04Week 7 onward

Deliver

A joint team, a plan of milestones rather than tasks, and a weekly forum that removes blockers. We track the metric from step one until it moves.

  • Joint delivery office
  • Weekly value tracking
  • Handover to your own leads
