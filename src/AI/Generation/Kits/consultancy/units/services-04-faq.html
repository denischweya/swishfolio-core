Before you ask

Straight answers.

If yours is not here, a partner will answer it within one working day.

Ask a partner
How does the fee at risk work?

In week one we agree a single metric, its baseline and who verifies it, usually your finance team. On programme engagements 15% of our fee is invoiced only once that metric has moved by the agreed amount. If it does not move, you keep it.

Who will actually be on the team?

The people you meet in the proposal. A partner leads every engagement and is with your team every week, not only at steering committees. We do not swap in juniors after signature.

Do you implement technology?

We design, prototype and take first models into production, and we lead delivery of large programmes. We do not sell licences or resell platforms, and we take no commission from any vendor.

How small an engagement will you take on?

A decision sprint is the smallest: four weeks, four people, one question. Below that we will usually point you to someone better suited.

Will you work alongside our existing advisers?

Yes, often. Around a third of our delivery work involves holding a systems integrator or another firm to a plan on your behalf.
