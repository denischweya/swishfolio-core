Ask Dan
How discreet is your work?

Completely. We work with a small number of private clients, and what you share is used only to advise you. Nothing is passed on without your agreement.

Will I work with Dan directly?

Yes. Dan Kimbo works personally with every client, from the first conversation onwards. You are not handed to a junior team.

What does purposeful investing mean in practice?

It means directing capital toward industries you consider important, with a clear view of risk and return. Purpose and discipline sit side by side.

Do you sell investment products?

No. We are an independent consultancy. Our role is to give you considered advice, not to place you in any particular product.

How does a relationship begin?

With a private email. Tell us a little about your situation, and we will arrange a conversation to see whether we can help.
