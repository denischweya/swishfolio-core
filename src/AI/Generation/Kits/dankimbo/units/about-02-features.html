Principles

Five rules we hold ourselves to.

They shape every conversation we have, and clients are welcome to hold us to them.

  1. 01

    Keep it discreet

    Your affairs stay private. We share nothing outside the conversation, and we work with a small number of clients.

  2. 02

    Give personal attention

    You deal directly with Dan Kimbo, not a rotating team. Advice is shaped around your own situation.

  3. 03

    Say what we think

    Honest advice includes the risks and the reasons not to act. We tell you plainly where we disagree.

  4. 04

    Think long term

    Wealth is built and passed on over decades. We weigh each decision against where you want to be later.

  5. 05

    Invest where it matters

    We help direct capital into industries that make a lasting difference, not only those that look good today.
