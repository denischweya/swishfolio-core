How we work

From first conversation to capital at work in four steps.

Every client follows the same clear path, whether the work is a single decision or a long relationship. You always know which step you are in and what you will have at the end of it.

Our services
01First step

Listen

We begin with your situation, your aims and what you want your wealth to achieve. If we are not the right fit, we say so before any work begins.

  • Private first conversation
  • Your goals in writing
  • Agreed risk appetite
02Second step

Research

We study the industries that matter to you and the opportunities within them, looking closely at the risks as well as the promise of each one.

  • Industries shortlisted
  • Opportunities weighed
  • Risks set out plainly
03Third step

Decide

We set out clear options with their risks named. We recommend one, explain why, and tell you what would change our view.

  • Options for your review
  • Balance of risk and liquidity
  • A written recommendation
04Ongoing

Steward

Once capital is placed, we stay involved with regular reviews, honest reporting and adjustments as your circumstances and aims change.

  • Regular review meetings
  • Honest, plain reporting
  • Adjustments as life changes
