Vesper started in 2016 in a former print shop in Roma Norte, with a borrowed projector and a belief that light is the most emotional material there is.

Ten years later we are 34 people across Mexico City and Copenhagen: directors, 3D artists, designers, engineers, producers and one very patient sound designer. We have stayed deliberately small, we have no account managers, and the people who pitch your project are the people who make it.

We are independent and owned by the people who work here. That lets us say no to work we cannot do well, and take time over the work we can.

  1. 2016

    Lucía Varela opens Vesper in a former print shop in Roma Norte, with two friends and one projector.

  2. 2019

    The first installation, Lumen Pavilion, travels to four cities and changes what we think we are.

  3. 2021

    Copenhagen opens in a canal-side warehouse in Nordhavn, led by Anders Holm.

  4. 2024

    The R&D lab and the 96-GPU render farm are built. Thursdays become experiment days.

  5. 2026

    34 people, two studios, one pipeline, and still no account managers.
