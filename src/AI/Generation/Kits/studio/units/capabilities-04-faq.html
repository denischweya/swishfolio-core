Questions

Good to know.

Anything else, ask a producer. They reply within two working days.

Do you work with agencies?

Often. Around half of our projects come through creative agencies who bring the idea and ask us to make it. We are happy to work white-label.

What is your smallest project?

A campaign sprint of six weeks is usually the smallest. For a single animated loop or still we will often recommend a freelancer we trust.

Can you install outside Mexico and Denmark?

Yes, we have installed in eleven countries. We travel with a core technical team and hire local crews we have worked with before.

Who owns the work?

You own the final deliverables outright. We keep the right to show the project in our portfolio once it has launched, and nothing before.

Do you use generative AI?

For research and early sketches, sometimes, and always with your agreement. Nothing we deliver is generated without a person making every final decision.
