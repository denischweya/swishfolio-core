The firm

We are large enough to run a £300m transaction and small enough that the partner who takes your first call is still there at completion, and at the next one.

Ashcombe Vale was founded on Queen Square in 1897 to act for the merchants of the Bristol docks. We still act for some of their descendants, and for the businesses that have grown up around the city and in London since.

We have stayed independent by choice. That means no referral targets, no cross-selling and no pressure to grow a matter beyond what it needs. It also means our partners answer to clients first.
