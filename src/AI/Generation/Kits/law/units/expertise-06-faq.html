Before you instruct us

Common questions.

If yours is not answered here, our enquiries team will put you in touch with the right partner.

Will a partner handle my matter?

A partner is responsible for every matter and leads the work personally on anything significant. Associates and trainees handle tasks where that is more cost-effective for you, under that partner’s supervision.

Why do you need to run a conflict check first?

Before we can hear the details of a matter we must check that we do not act for anyone on the other side. It usually takes a few hours and is why our first reply asks only for names.

Do you act outside Bristol and London?

Yes. Clients are throughout England and Wales, and about a fifth of our work involves another jurisdiction, where we work with trusted firms abroad.

Can you work alongside our in-house team?

Often. We regularly act as an extension of in-house legal and HR teams, and can second lawyers for defined projects.
