Good to know

Before you write

How soon will I hear back?

Within two working days, usually sooner. If I am away, an automatic reply will say when I am back at the desk.

What does a project usually cost?

Identity projects typically start at 6,000 euros, posters at 1,500 euros and packaging ranges at 8,000 euros. After a short call I send a fixed quote, so there are no surprises.

Do you work with clients outside Austria?

Yes. About half of my clients are elsewhere in Europe. We meet on video, and I handle print production remotely with printers I trust.

Can you work with my existing logo?

Often, yes. Many projects start with a refresh rather than a new mark: tightening the logotype, choosing better type and building the system around it.
