About

I have been drawing letters since I could hold a pencil.

I grew up above my parents’ print shop in Graz, folding leaflets after school and learning early that a good layout saves paper and patience. I studied visual communication in Linz, then spent eight years in studios in Copenhagen, Vienna and Berlin.

Since 2022 I have worked independently from a shared studio in Neubau. Most of my clients are small: founders, publishers, cinemas and cafés who want something that feels made rather than generated. I keep the practice deliberately small too, so the person you brief is the person who designs.

Away from the desk I collect matchbox labels, run a type reading group once a month and cycle to the printer whenever I can.
