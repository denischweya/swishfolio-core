Pricing questions

About the money.

Is there a contract or minimum term?

No minimum term. Plans run month to month and you can leave with thirty days’ notice. We hand everything over to your next accountant without fuss.

What if my business grows mid-year?

We review your plan every twelve months. If you outgrow it sooner, we will suggest a change and you decide; we never move you up without asking.

Is there a set-up fee?

Not for switching from another accountant. If your books are more than three months behind, catch-up work is quoted separately at £60 per month brought up to date.

Do you offer a discount for paying annually?

Yes: pay for twelve months up front and the thirteenth is free.
