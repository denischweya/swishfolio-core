Questions

The things people ask on the first call.

If yours isn’t here, ask us. We answer every email within one working day.

Ask a question
Can you take over part-way through the year?

Yes, and most clients join that way. We request a handover from your previous accountant, pick up from your last filed return and make sure nothing falls between the two.

Do I have to change my accounting software?

No. We work in Xero, QuickBooks, FreeAgent and Sage. If you are on spreadsheets we will suggest the simplest option and set it up for you.

Will you deal with HMRC for me?

Yes. Once you authorise us as your agent, HMRC letters and calls come to us. We tell you what they say and what, if anything, you need to do.

What if I need something not in my plan?

We quote it in writing before doing any work. Common extras such as an R&D claim or a capital gains computation have a published fixed price.
