How we work

Four promises we put in writing.

They are in every letter of engagement. If we break one, tell us, and tell us what you think it should cost us.

  1. 01

    Fixed means fixed

    The fee you agree is the fee you pay for twelve months. If we underquoted, that is our problem.

  2. 02

    Plain English

    No jargon in emails. If we have to use a technical term, we explain it in the same sentence.

  3. 03

    One working day

    Every question gets a real answer within one working day, from someone who knows your file.

  4. 04

    Early, not on time

    We aim to file every return at least a week before it is due. Deadlines are for emergencies.
