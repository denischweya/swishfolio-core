A typical month

What actually happens after you sign up.

No black box. You can see the work in your software and in a shared checklist, and you always know what is coming next.

  1. 1st

    Bank feeds reconciled

    Every transaction from last month matched and categorised.

  2. 7th

    Management pack

    Profit, cash and the tax you should be setting aside, in one page.

  3. 15th

    Check-in

    Fifteen minutes with your accountant, if you want it. Most months you won’t need it.

  4. 22nd

    PAYE paid

    Payroll run, RTI submitted and your PAYE bill paid to HMRC on time.
