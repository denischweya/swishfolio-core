Questions

The practical bits.

How far ahead do you book?

Retainers usually start four to six weeks after the discovery call. Strategy Sprints can often begin within two weeks.

Do you work with very small brands?

Yes. The Strategy Sprint was designed for founders who post themselves and want a plan they can follow without a monthly retainer.

Can we meet in person?

If you are in Toronto, gladly: I am happy to meet at your space or over coffee on Ossington. Everyone else gets a video call.
