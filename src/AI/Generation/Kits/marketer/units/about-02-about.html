I fell for social media the first time a comment I answered turned into a sale.

That was 2017, at a tiny clean-skincare startup where I was the entire marketing team. I learned to shoot product photos on a windowsill, write captions people actually read and answer every message like it came from a friend.

Agency life taught me strategy, budgets and how to run twelve brands at once. Going independent in 2021 let me do the part I love most: working closely with founders, being hands-on with the content and seeing exactly what it does for the business.

Today I keep a small roster on purpose. Four retainer brands at a time, a video editor I trust completely and a network of creators I have worked with for years.
