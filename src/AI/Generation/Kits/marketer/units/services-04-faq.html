Questions

Before you book.

Anything else? Ask on the discovery call. There is no such thing as a silly question about social.

Which platforms do you work on?

Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Threads and YouTube Shorts. Most brands do best focusing on two platforms properly rather than six half-heartedly, and the strategy tells us which two.

Do we have to be on camera?

Not unless you want to be. Plenty of my best-performing content features products, hands, customers or your team. If you do want to show up, I will coach you through it so it never feels forced.

How soon will we see results?

Engagement usually moves within the first month. Follower growth and sales take a full quarter to show a reliable trend, which is why retainers start with a three-month term.

Is ad spend included in the price?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to the platforms from your own account, so you always own the data and the ad history. My fee covers strategy, creative, setup and weekly optimisation.

Do you work with brands outside Toronto?

Yes. About half my clients are elsewhere in Canada, the US or the UK. Shoot days happen locally or with a trusted creator near you.
