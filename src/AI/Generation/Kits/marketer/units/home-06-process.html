How I work

From first call to first results in four steps.

A clear rhythm so you always know what is happening, what is next and what it is doing for the business.

  1. 01

    Week 1

    Listen & audit

    A discovery call, a look at your channels and three competitors, and short interviews with real customers.

  2. 02

    Weeks 2–3

    Strategy & pillars

    Goals for every platform, content pillars, your brand voice and a 90-day calendar we agree together.

  3. 03

    Month 1

    Create & launch

    A batch shoot day, scheduled posts, community replies and, if you want them, the first paid tests.

  4. 04

    Every month

    Measure & grow

    A one-page report, three changes for next month, and budget moved towards what works.
