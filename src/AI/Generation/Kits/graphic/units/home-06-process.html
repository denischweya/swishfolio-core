How it works

Four steps, printed as posters.

A typical identity takes ten weeks. You always know which poster we’re on.

Week 1–2Oddform process

01

Listen

A workshop, a lot of questions and a walk around your shop, studio or warehouse. I want to know what you’d never change.

Week 3–4Oddform process

02

Find the idea

One strong concept, presented on real things (a bag, a sign, an Instagram post) not floating on a white slide.

Week 5–8Oddform process

03

Build the system

Logo, colour, type, layouts and rules, tested on every touchpoint until nothing feels like an afterthought.

Week 9–10Oddform process

04

Hand over

Print-ready files, templates and a guide written for your team, plus a check-in three months after launch.
