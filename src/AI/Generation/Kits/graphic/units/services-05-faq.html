Good questions

Before you ask.

Ask me anything
Why only one concept?

Because I do the exploring for you. I develop dozens of routes internally and present the one I believe in, with the reasons. If it misses, the second round is on me.

Do you work with clients outside the Netherlands?

Yes, about a third of my clients are in Belgium, Germany, Scandinavia and the UK. Workshops happen on video, and I travel for the important ones.

Can you build our website too?

I design websites as part of an identity and work with a trusted developer, Ilse de Wit, to build them. She quotes separately so you always see what each part costs.

Who owns the work?

You do. On final payment you receive full ownership of the logo and identity, and an unlimited licence for any custom typeface.

How far ahead do you book?

Usually eight to ten weeks. For packaging with a fixed print date, get in touch as early as you can.
