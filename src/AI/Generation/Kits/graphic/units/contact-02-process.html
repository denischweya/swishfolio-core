What happens next

From first hello to kick‑off in a week.

  1. 01

    You send a brief

    A few sentences is enough. Attach anything you like: old logos, competitors, a photo of the shop.

  2. 02

    We have a call

    Thirty minutes, free, on video or in the studio. I ask a lot of questions and tell you honestly whether I’m the right fit.

  3. 03

    You get a proposal

    Within three days: the scope, a timeline with real dates and one fixed fee. No hourly surprises.
