About the studio

Hoi! I’m Noor, and Oddform is my one-person studio.

I design identities, packaging and printed matter for independent businesses and cultural organisations, from a light-filled studio in Delfshaven, Rotterdam.

Oddform is deliberately small. I take on around eight projects a year and do the design myself, start to finish. When a project needs photography, copy or code, I bring in a crew of people I’ve worked with for years.
