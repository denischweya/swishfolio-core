// how i work

Three steps, no black boxes.

01 · scope

One week, paid, cancellable

I read the code, talk to whoever has to live with the result, and write down what we are building, what we are deliberately not building, and what it costs. If the answer is that you do not need me, I will say so.

02 · build in the open

A demo every Friday

You get commit access, a shared board and a preview environment per pull request from day one. Weekly demo, written notes, and an honest line about anything that slipped. No sprint ends in a surprise.

03 · hand over

You own it, not me

Tests, docs, runbook and a recorded walkthrough of every moving part. Then two weeks where I am still on the hook for anything that breaks. No retainer required afterwards, though plenty of clients keep one.
