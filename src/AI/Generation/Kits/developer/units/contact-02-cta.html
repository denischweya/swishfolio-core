// what to put in the message

Four lines are enough.

  • What the product does, in one sentence a stranger would understand
  • What is broken, missing or too slow right now
  • The stack it runs on, and anything you cannot change about it
  • The date it has to work by, and roughly what you have to spend
  • A repo link if you can share one. I will read it before we talk

Not a recruiter pitch, not a "quick call to explore synergies". Specifics get answered fastest.

availability
