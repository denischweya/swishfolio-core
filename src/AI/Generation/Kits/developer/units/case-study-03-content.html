01The problem

A nightly file, and a lot of hoping.

Tallo paid its sellers through a CSV file uploaded to the bank every night. When it worked, sellers waited two days for their money. When it didn’t, nobody knew until a seller wrote in, usually at month end, when volumes tripled.

Balances lived in a column on the sellers table, updated in place. There was no history to audit, so every Monday the operations team reconciled the bank statement by hand.
