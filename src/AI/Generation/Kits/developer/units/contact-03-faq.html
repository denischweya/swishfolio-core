03Questions

The practical bits.

How far ahead do you book?

Builds usually start four to eight weeks after the call. Audits and short performance projects can sometimes begin within two weeks.

Do you work with early-stage startups?

Yes, if there is a clear problem and someone who owns the product. For very early teams the audit or a short build is often the best fit.

Can we meet in person?

If you’re in Montréal, gladly: coffee in Mile End is on me. Everyone else gets a video call and a shared doc.
