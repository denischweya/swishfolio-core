Rebuilding marketplace payouts so sellers get paid in minutes

Problem
Payouts sent as a nightly bank file: two-day waits, silent failures and manual reconciliation every Monday.
Build
A double-entry ledger, an idempotent payouts engine in Go and a transactional outbox to every bank rail.
Result
Median payout in four minutes, zero ledger mismatches and on-call pages down from thirty a month to two.
