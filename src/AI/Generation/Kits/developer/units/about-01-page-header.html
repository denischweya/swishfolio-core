Kai Okafor

Full-stack engineer, Berlin. Nine years across fintech, healthcare and commerce, half of it inside product teams, half of it as the person they call when the system has outgrown itself.

I started writing code at fourteen, on a secondhand ThinkPad, mostly to make a browser game load faster than my friend's. The instinct never really changed: I am interested in the part of the work where something measurable gets better: a page that renders in 900ms instead of 6.4 seconds, an on-call rota that stops ruining weekends, a deploy that nobody has to be awake for.

Since 2024 I have worked independently, usually on two clients at a time so each one gets real attention. In between I maintain Orbit, mentor three junior developers through a Berlin apprenticeship scheme, and give the occasional meetup talk about why your p99 is lying to you.

Away from the keyboard I am on a bike, generally further from home than planned.
