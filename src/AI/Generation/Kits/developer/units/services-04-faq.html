The questions I always get.

Anything not covered here, ask it directly. I would rather answer a hard question before the contract than after.

Do you work with existing teams or only solo?

Both, and the contract engagement exists precisely for the first case. On Tandem I led four other engineers; on Gridwise I am the only person on the ingest path. What I will not do is work around a team, if your engineers own the code, they review my pull requests like anyone else's.

What happens if the build runs over?

Fixed-price work stays fixed-price. If I misjudged the estimate, that is my cost to carry, and it has happened twice in nine years. If the scope changes because you learned something, which is normal and usually good, we price the change as its own small piece of work before anyone starts on it.

Will you work in our stack rather than yours?

Yes, within reason. I am fluent in Go, TypeScript, Python, Rust and PHP, so most stacks are fine. I will tell you honestly if a technology choice is going to cost you later, but I will not rewrite a working Rails app in Go because I prefer Go.

Who owns the code and the IP?

You do, assigned on final payment, in writing. The only exception is general-purpose tooling that ends up in Orbit or another of my open-source projects, which stays Apache-2.0 and which you keep the right to use regardless. Nothing client-specific ever leaves your repository.
