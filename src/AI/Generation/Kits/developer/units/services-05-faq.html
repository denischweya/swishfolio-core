04Questions

Before you book.

Anything else? Ask on the discovery call. I’d rather answer a question now than surprise you later.

Do you work with existing codebases?

Most of the time, yes. Plenty of my best projects started with a codebase that needed care rather than a rewrite. The audit is often the right first step.

Will my team be able to maintain what you build?

That’s the point. I use mainstream tools, write tests and documentation as I go, and pair with your engineers so the knowledge stays with you.

Do you sign NDAs and work in our accounts?

Yes. Code lives in your repository and runs in your cloud account from the first day. I’m happy to sign a mutual NDA before the discovery call.

Can you start next week?

Usually not. Builds are booked four to eight weeks ahead. Audits and short performance projects can sometimes start sooner.

Do you work with clients outside Canada?

Yes, about half my clients are in the US and Europe. Montréal’s time zone overlaps comfortably with both.
