(Process)

Twelve weeks,
five moves

The same rhythm on every project, scaled up or down. Nothing disappears for a month and comes back as a reveal.

01

Week 1

Listen

Workshops with the people who run the business and the people who use it. We leave with a one-page brief everyone has signed.

02

Weeks 2–3

Frame

Positioning, audit and two or three strategic routes. You pick a direction before a single pixel is polished.

03

Weeks 3–9

Make

Design and build in weekly loops. You see work every Friday, in the browser or on a phone, never in a slide deck.

04

Weeks 9–12

Launch

QA, content, training and a launch plan. We are in the room on the day, and we stay on call for the first month.

05

Ongoing

Grow

Quarterly reviews against the numbers we agreed at the start. Most clients keep a small retainer for what comes next.
