(How we think)

Four habits
we won't break

01

Show it early

Rough work on day four beats a perfect deck in week six. You see everything while it can still change.

02

Make it move

Brands live on screens. If an idea only works as a static logo on white, it isn't finished.

03

Stay small

Twelve people is a choice. It means the person who sold the work is the person doing it.

04

Count it

We agree the numbers that matter before we start and check them a year later, in writing.
