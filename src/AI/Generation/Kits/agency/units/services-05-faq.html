Fair
questions

Not covered here? Email hello@ozmo.studio and a human answers, usually Yara.

How much does a project cost?

Most projects land between €48,000 and €160,000 depending on scope. A two-week sprint starts at €14,000. We quote a fixed fee per phase, so you always know the next number before you commit to it.

How long does it take?

A brand identity is typically eight to ten weeks, a marketing site ten to fourteen, and product work runs in quarters. We can move faster with a sprint if there is a real deadline, like a raise or a launch.

Who will we actually work with?

The people on the Studio page. A design lead, a producer and one of the two founders are on every project from first call to launch. We don't have account managers and we don't hand work to juniors once it is sold.

Do you work with clients outside the Netherlands?

About 60% of our work is for clients abroad, in fourteen countries so far. We work in English and Dutch, keep overlap hours with US East Coast time, and travel for kickoffs and launches.

Can you work with our in-house team?

Happily. Around half our projects are run alongside an internal design or engineering team. We agree who owns what in week one and we build in your tools, your repo and your design system where they exist.

What do you need from us to start?

A decision-maker who can join a weekly review, access to your customers, and honest numbers. If you don't have a written brief, the form on the Contact page is enough to begin.
