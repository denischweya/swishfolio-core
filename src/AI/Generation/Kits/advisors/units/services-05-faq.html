Are you consultants or coaches?

A bit of both, which is why we call ourselves advisors. We bring proven tools and hands-on experience of running businesses, and we coach you and your team to use them. We don’t write a report and disappear.

How big does my business need to be?

Most clients have between 10 and 250 people and revenue between $1m and $40m. Below that, our Clarity Sprint can still be a good fit; above it, we’ll tell you honestly if a larger firm would serve you better.

Do you work outside Austin?

Yes. About half of our clients are elsewhere in Texas and the South. We do planning days in person and monthly sessions by video.

Can we stop if it isn’t working?

Monthly programmes run on thirty days’ notice after the first three months. If we aren’t adding clear value, we would rather you stopped.

Will you work with my accountant and lawyer?

Always. We complement them rather than replace them, and on exit work we coordinate the whole advisory team.
