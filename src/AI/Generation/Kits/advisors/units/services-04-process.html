Your first 90 days

What actually happens after you say yes.

No months of analysis before anything changes. By the end of the first quarter you’ll have a plan, a scorecard and the first priorities already moving.

  1. Week 1

    Kick-off

    Half a day with you: the story so far, where you want to go and what keeps you up at night.

  2. Weeks 2–3

    Diagnosis

    Interviews with your key people, time on the floor and a deep look at your numbers.

  3. Week 4

    Planning day

    Your leadership team builds the one-page plan and agrees the first ninety-day priorities.

  4. Month 2

    First board

    Your first monthly advisory board: progress, numbers and the hardest decision on your desk.

  5. Month 3

    Reset

    A quarterly reset day to review the ninety days, celebrate wins and set the next priorities.
