About Oakline

Advisors who have run businesses, for people who run businesses.

Oakline was founded in Austin in 2009 by Rosa Delgado-Whitfield, after fourteen years running, and then selling, her family’s building-supply company. She started the firm she wished she’d had: senior, practical, and on the owner’s side of the table.
