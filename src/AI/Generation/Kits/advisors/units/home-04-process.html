The Oakline method

Three steps from stuck to steady.

Every engagement follows the same spine. You always know which step you’re in, what you’ll have at the end of it and what it costs.

  1. 1

    Weeks 1–3

    Diagnose

    We spend time with you, your team and your numbers (on the floor, not just in the boardroom) and score the business across growth, operations, team and cash.

    • Health-check scorecard
    • Interviews with your key people
    • Your real margin, by product and customer
  2. 2

    Week 4

    Plan

    One page, not a binder. Where the business should be in three years, the five priorities that get it there, and who owns each one for the next ninety days.

    • One-page strategic plan
    • 90-day priorities with owners
    • A scorecard of 10 numbers
  3. 3

    Month 2 onward

    Lead

    We become your advisory board: monthly sessions with you and your leaders, quarterly planning days, and a call whenever something big lands on your desk.

    • Monthly advisory board
    • Quarterly reset day
    • Unlimited calls & email

Quarterly reset day. Your leadership team, a whiteboard and a full day away from the phones. Most clients say it’s the most useful day of their quarter.
