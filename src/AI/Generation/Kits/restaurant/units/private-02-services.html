The spaces

Three ways to gather

Every booking gets its own host, a menu printed with your names on it and the fire lit an hour early.

The Hearth Table

8–10 guests

One oak table set three steps from the fire. The cooks pass dishes straight across and talk you through them. Loud, warm and a bit smoky.

  • Feast menu from £65
  • No hire fee Tue–Thu
  • Step-free

The Wine Room

12–22 guests

Downstairs, behind the bottle racks, with its own door, its own playlist and a brick vault ceiling. Seated at one long table or two.

  • Minimum spend £1,400
  • Own sound & screen
  • Until midnight

The Whole House

Up to 70 guests

Every table, the counter and the yard. Forty-six seated for a feast, or seventy standing with plates from the fire going round all night.

  • From £4,800 Sun–Thu
  • Late licence to 1am
  • Band or DJ welcome
