How we work

Three rules we wrote on the back of the first lease and have not had to change.

01 · Whole

Use all of it.

Fish bones become pil pil. Leek tops become oil. Yesterday’s bread becomes ajo blanco. Our food waste fits in one small caddy a night.

02 · Near

Buy it close.

Eighty per cent of what we cook is grown, raised or landed within fifty miles. The wine travels further; we make up for it with sherry.

03 · Fair

Pay it properly.

A four-day week for the whole team, every penny of service shared evenly, and the kitchen eats together at 4.30pm.
