In the kitchen

Inés Carvalho

Inés grew up between her grandmother’s kitchen in Porto and a terraced house in Bedminster, and has been trying to reconcile the two ever since. She spent a decade in grill kitchens on the Basque coast and in London before coming home to light her own fire.

  • 2009First kitchen job, washing pans in a harbourside fish restaurant.
  • 2013Moves to the Basque coast to learn the grill. Stays four years.
  • 2019The trailer years: two summers of sardines on the harbour wall.
  • 2021Lumbre opens at 14 Tannery Row with Tom Adeyemi on the floor.
  • 2026No. 07 in the West Country Table Top 50. Still no gas line.
