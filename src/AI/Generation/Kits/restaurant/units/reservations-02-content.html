Finding us

Follow the woodsmoke.

Look for the brick chimney and the tomato-red door, halfway along Tannery Row between the footbridge and the old crane.

On foot

12 minutes from Temple Meads

Cross the footbridge at Old Quay, turn left at the crane and keep the water on your right. Step-free from the bridge to the door.

By ferry or bus

Old Quay landing

The harbour ferry stops forty metres from the door until 10.15pm. Buses 8 and 72 stop on Tanners Bridge Road.

By car or bike

Quayside car park

Two minutes away on Hide Street, open until 1am. Twelve bike hoops outside the yard gate; we will lend you a lock.
