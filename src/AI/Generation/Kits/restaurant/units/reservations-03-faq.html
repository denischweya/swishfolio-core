Good to know

House rules, gently.

The short version: come hungry, tell us about allergies, and let us know if plans change.

How long do we have the table?

Two hours for tables of up to four, two and a quarter for five and six. The Long Fire tasting menu has the table for the evening. If nobody is booked after you, stay as long as you like.

Running late or need to cancel?

We hold tables for fifteen minutes. Cancel or change free of charge up to 48 hours ahead; after that, tables of five or more and all tasting-menu bookings are charged £20 per guest, which we waive if we re-fill the table.

Groups of seven or more

Larger groups eat from a feast menu at the Hearth Table or in the Wine Room. Have a look at private dining, or email events@lumbre.example and Maya will sort you out.

Allergies and dietary needs

Tell us when you book and again when you sit down. We can cook around most things, but everything shares one fire, so we cannot promise any dish is entirely free from an allergen.

Children and dogs

Children are welcome at every service; there is a shorter menu and two high chairs. Well-behaved dogs are welcome at the counter and in the yard, and there are biscuits behind the bar.

Service charge and gift cards

A discretionary 12.5% is added to the bill and shared evenly between the whole team, kitchen included. Gift cards are sold at the bar and by email, and never expire.
