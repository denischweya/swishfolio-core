The house

Lumbre is the Spanish word for the glow a fire gives off once the flames have settled. That is the heat we cook on, and the kind of evening we are trying to make.

Everything on the menu passes through the hearth: hung above it, buried in its ash, or laid straight on the coals. The kitchen is open, the counter faces the fire, and the cooks bring the plates to the table themselves.

We write the menu at four o’clock, once the day boats have landed at Newlyn and Hollow Oak Farm has dropped off the crates. It is short on purpose. Order a few things, share them, stay a while.

Inés CarvalhoChef & co-owner
