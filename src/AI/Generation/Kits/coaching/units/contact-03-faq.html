Questions

The practical bits.

Where is the quiet room?

A first-floor room in Stockbridge, ten minutes’ walk from Princes Street. Directions and the door code come with your booking confirmation.

What should I bring to a walking session?

Comfortable shoes and a waterproof. We walk at conversation pace and sessions go ahead in most weather; if it is truly wild we move indoors.

Do you work in other languages?

Yes. I coach in English and Swedish, and I am happy to take sessions in Norwegian or Danish if you are patient with my accent.
