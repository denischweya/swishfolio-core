How engagements work

Diagnose, design, deliver

Most engagements run between six and twelve weeks. You'll always know what I'm doing, what it costs and what you'll have at the end of it.

1

Two weeks of looking

Interviews, numbers and time spent watching how the work really happens. I report back on the problem I found, which is often not the one in the brief.

2

Design it together

Workshops with the people who'll live with the result. Solutions your team helped build are the only ones that survive my leaving.

3

Stay through delivery

A day a fortnight while the change beds in, coaching the managers carrying it. Then a handover and a date to check back in.
