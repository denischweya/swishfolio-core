How it goes

Four steps, no surprises

1

A free call

Thirty minutes to talk about where you are and what you want to be different. No pitch, no preparation.

2

A written plan

One page: what we're working on, how often we meet, what it costs and how we'll know it worked.

3

The work

Regular sessions, honest reflection and small experiments between them. You can message me in between.

4

A clean ending

We review against what you set out to change. Good coaching ends; I'd rather you didn't need me.
