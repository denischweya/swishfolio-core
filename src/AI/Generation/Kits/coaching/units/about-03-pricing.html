What I believe

Good leaders are made. Almost nobody arrives knowing how to do this, and the ones who look like they do have usually just had more help.

Kindness and candour are the same thing

Letting someone carry on without telling them the truth isn't gentle, it's just more comfortable for me. I'll always say the thing, and I'll say it carefully.

Small, repeated changes beat big plans

One different conversation a week changes a team faster than a transformation programme. We'll work at that scale, and it will feel almost too easy at first.

The goal is not needing me

Coaching that runs forever has usually stopped being coaching. We agree what we're here to change, and we finish when it has changed.
