About

I ran the job before I started coaching people who do it.

I spent ten years in operations, the last four as COO of a four-hundred-person hospitality group. I know what it is to make a decision at eleven at night with incomplete information and forty people's jobs attached to it.

I left in 2016 to train properly as a coach, because the part of the job I'd always been best at was the half-hour conversation that unlocked somebody else's week. Since then I've coached more than four hundred leaders and worked with organisations across technology, healthcare, retail and hospitality.

I take on a small number of clients at a time, deliberately. Coaching is not a volume business, and neither is consulting when you plan to be in the building.
