For twenty years I was the steady one in the room. Then I realised I had never once been asked what I actually wanted.

I spent my first career in operations, most of it in Edinburgh, the last five years as a chief operating officer. I was good at it. I was also tired in a way a holiday did not fix, and I noticed I was having the same conversation over and over with the leaders around me: late, quietly, after the meeting.

A coach helped me see that those conversations were the part of the job I loved. In 2014 I left, retrained in coaching psychology and started my own practice from a borrowed room above a bookshop.

Twelve years on, I coach executives, founders and people at a crossroads, and design leadership programmes for organisations who want braver, calmer leaders. I still walk to work most days.

  1. 2004
    Moved to Edinburgh

    Arrived from Gothenburg for a two-year operations contract. Stayed for the light, the hills and, eventually, a family.

  2. 2009
    Chief operating officer

    Led 400 people through growth, a merger and one very hard year. Learnt most of what I know about leadership by getting it wrong first.

  3. 2014
    Retrained as a coach

    MSc in Coaching Psychology and ICF accreditation. Started coaching leaders I used to sit beside in board meetings.

  4. 2019
    Walk and talk

    Began taking sessions outdoors, and watched how differently people think side by side and on the move.

  5. 2026
    600+ clients

    Executives, founders and people in transition, plus leadership programmes for more than forty organisations.
