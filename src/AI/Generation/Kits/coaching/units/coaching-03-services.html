Who this is for

This will probably help if…

  • You've been promoted into a job nobody trained you for.
  • You're the person everyone brings problems to, and you've run out of places to take your own.
  • You know what the hard conversation is. You've known for months.
  • Your calendar is full and your priorities are not.

It probably won't if…

  • You want someone to tell you that you're right.
  • Someone else has decided you need coaching and you haven't.
  • You're looking for therapy. I'll help you find the right person instead.
  • You can't protect an hour a fortnight. That's the minimum this takes.
