How it works

Four steps, no surprises.

A simple shape, so you always know where we are, what comes next and what it is costing you.

  1. 01

    30 minutes

    A free call

    We talk about what is going on and what you want to change. If I’m not the right coach, I’ll say so and suggest someone who is.

  2. 02

    Session one

    Shape the work

    We agree two or three goals, how we will know they are met and, for executives, who else should have a say.

  3. 03

    Every two to three weeks

    The sessions

    Honest conversation, practical tools and one real step at the end of each session. Message me in between when it matters.

  4. 04

    Final session

    A clean ending

    We look back at the goals, name what changed and plan how you keep it going. Good coaching ends; I’d rather you didn’t need me.
