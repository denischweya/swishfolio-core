My approach

Coaching isn’t advice. It’s a quiet room where you finally hear yourself think.

I trained in coaching psychology after twenty years running operations teams, so I know the pressure you are under from the inside. Our sessions are warm, honest and practical, and they always end with something you will do.

  1. 01

    Slow down

    Sessions are unhurried on purpose. Clarity rarely arrives in a thirty-minute slot between meetings.

  2. 02

    Say the true thing

    I’ll be warm and I’ll be direct. You get my honest read, not a framework recited back at you.

  3. 03

    Act this week

    Every session ends with one small, real step. Insight only counts once it changes a Tuesday.
