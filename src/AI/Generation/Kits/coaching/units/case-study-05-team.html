The outcome

Faster decisions, and a team people want to join.

Within a month of the offsite the team had agreed the new office and the engineering restructure. By the end of the engagement the weekly leadership meeting had shrunk from three hours to seventy-five minutes, with a written decision log nobody had to chase.

The annual staff survey showed engagement up eighteen points, with the biggest jump on “I trust leadership to make clear decisions”. Two of the seven leaders now coach managers inside the practice.

  • Decision charter on one page
  • Monthly feedback round, still running
  • Two internal coaches trained
  • Chief executive coaching continued for six months
