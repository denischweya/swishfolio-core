Who I work with

Capable people, at the moments that ask more of them.

Most of my clients are doing well on paper. They come because something has shifted: a bigger role, a company that outgrew them, or a quiet sense that the life they built no longer fits.

Leaders stepping up

“Everyone thinks I’ve got this. Most days I’m not sure I have.”

New to the C-suite, newly promoted or newly responsible for people who used to be your peers. We build the presence and judgement the role asks for, without losing what got you here.

Founders carrying it all

“The company grew up. I’m not sure my job did.”

You built it, and now it needs a different version of you. We untangle what only you can do, what you need to let go of, and how to lead a team that is bigger than your inbox.

People at a crossroads

“I’m good at my job. I just don’t want it any more.”

A career that no longer fits, a big decision you keep postponing, or a life that changed shape. We make room to think, test the options in real life, and choose deliberately.
