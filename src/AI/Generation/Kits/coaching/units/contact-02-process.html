The discovery call

What actually happens on it

1

You talk, I listen

Twenty minutes on where you are and what's hard about it. Nothing to prepare. Most people arrive not quite knowing how to describe it, and that's fine.

2

We test the fit

I'll ask a few questions and you'll get a feel for how I work. If someone else would serve you better, I'll give you two names.

3

A one-page plan

If it feels right, I'll send a short proposal within two days: what we'd work on, how often we'd meet and what it costs. No pressure to answer quickly.
