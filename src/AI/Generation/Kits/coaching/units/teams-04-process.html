How an engagement runs

Listen first. Then design. Then do the work.

A clear rhythm for sponsors and participants alike, with a short written update every month.

  1. Weeks 1 to 3

    Listen

    Confidential interviews with leaders and a sample of staff, so we work on the real problem, not the presenting one.

  2. Week 4

    Design

    A short proposal with the shape of the work, what success looks like and how we will measure it.

  3. Months 2 to 7

    Work

    Workshops, team sessions and one-to-one coaching, adjusted every month to what is actually happening.

  4. Final month

    Embed

    Rituals, charters and internal champions, so the change keeps going after I leave.
