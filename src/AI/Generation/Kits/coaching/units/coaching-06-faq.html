Questions

Good questions to ask a coach.

Anything else, ask on the free call. There is no such thing as a silly question about coaching.

Is coaching the same as therapy?

No. Coaching looks forward and works on goals you choose; therapy treats mental health conditions and the past in a way coaching should not. If something comes up that needs a therapist, I will say so and help you find a good one.

Can my employer pay?

Often, yes. About two thirds of my executive clients are sponsored by their organisation. I can send a short proposal for your manager or HR team, and what we discuss stays between us.

How do I know you are the right coach?

That is what the free call is for. Chemistry matters more than credentials, so please talk to two or three coaches before you choose. I will not chase you afterwards.

What if I need to move a session?

Life happens. Give me 48 hours’ notice and we will simply rearrange. Programmes can pause for up to a month for holidays or illness.

Do you offer reduced rates?

Yes. I keep two places a year at a reduced rate for people working in charities, the arts or between jobs. Ask on the call.
