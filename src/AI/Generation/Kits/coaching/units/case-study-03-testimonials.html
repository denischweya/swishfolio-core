The starting point

Talented people, polite meetings, no decisions.

Fiona had been chief executive for eight months when she called. Her leadership team of seven were individually brilliant, and collectively stuck: three-hour meetings where everyone agreed, followed by weeks of corridor conversations where nobody did.

Two big decisions, a new office and a restructure of the engineering group, had been “nearly agreed” for most of a year. Good people were starting to leave.

“We were the nicest leadership team in Scotland, and we were slowly killing the business.”

Fiona R., Chief Executive
