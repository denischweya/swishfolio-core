Questions

Things people ask me first

What actually happens in a session?

You arrive with whatever is loudest. We spend the first ten minutes finding out what is really going on underneath it, then most of the hour working on that. We finish by agreeing one or two things you'll try before we next meet, and I write them down and send them to you.

Is it confidential if my company is paying?

Yes. Your sponsor gets a three-line note at the start and end of the engagement confirming the themes we agreed to work on, nothing more. I'll show you that note before it goes anywhere. Nothing you say in a session is ever repeated.

How is coaching different from mentoring or therapy?

A mentor tells you what they did. A therapist works with your past. Coaching sits in the present: it's your thinking, better organised, with someone who asks good questions and keeps you honest. I do bring operating experience when it's useful, and I'll always say when I'm doing that.

What if it isn't working?

Tell me, and we'll fix it or stop. There's a proper review at session four of every programme for exactly this reason. If we stop there, you pay for what we've used and nothing else.
