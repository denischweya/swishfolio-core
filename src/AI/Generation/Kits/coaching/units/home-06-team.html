75 minleadership meeting, down from three hours

Case study · Harbour & Co

A leadership team that learnt to disagree well.

Starting point
Seven talented leaders who agreed in meetings and disagreed in corridors. Decisions stalled for months.
Approach
Listening interviews, two days in the hills, monthly team coaching and a decision charter they wrote themselves.
Outcome
Meetings cut from three hours to seventy-five minutes, and staff engagement up eighteen points.
