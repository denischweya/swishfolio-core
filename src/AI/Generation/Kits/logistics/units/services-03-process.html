How a shipment moves

Six steps, one reference number.

From the first quote to the final proof of delivery, everything about a shipment lives under one Meridian reference you can track.

  1. Step 01Quote

    Priced within 2 working hours, all-in, valid 14 days.

  2. Step 02Book

    Online, by email or EDI. You get a Meridian reference straight away.

  3. Step 03Collect

    Booked slot at your dock, driver details sent the day before.

  4. Step 04Clear

    Export and import declarations filed by our own brokers.

  5. Step 05Move

    Tracked by the control tower, with exceptions flagged within 15 minutes.

  6. Step 06Deliver

    Slot confirmed with the receiver, ePOD and CO₂e on the invoice.
