Our story

Still privately owned. Still answering the phone.

Meridian was founded in 1998 by Pieter Maas, a harbour pilot who was tired of watching cargo wait on the quay because nobody had told the truck. The company has grown to 4,800 people in 14 countries, but the habit he started (tell the customer first, and tell them the truth) is still how every desk is measured.

We are owned by the Maas family and a foundation of long-serving employees. That means no quarterly targets to hit at the expense of a customer, and the patience to invest in electric trucks and warehouses that will pay back over decades.

  1. 1998

    Three trucks and a yard in Waalhaven. Founded by Pieter Maas, a former harbour pilot.

  2. 2006

    First warehouse opens in Venlo. Ocean forwarding desk added.

  3. 2011

    AEO-F certification. Customs brokerage becomes its own business line.

  4. 2015

    Offices in Chicago and Singapore. The control tower goes 24/7.

  5. 2021

    First 40 electric trucks. Emissions reporting on every invoice.

  6. 2026

    4,800 people, 23 warehouses, 310 electric trucks, still privately owned.
