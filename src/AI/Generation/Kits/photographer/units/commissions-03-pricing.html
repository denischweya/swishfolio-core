Where budgets usually land

Every commission is quoted individually. These ranges cover most of what was booked last year, including licensing but not travel.

Portrait sitting

€950–1,600

Studio or location, single subject, editorial or personal use.

Editorial story

€3,600–7,200

Two to four shooting days, edit, scans and first-use licence.

Hospitality library

€5,500–12,000

Two to five days on site, full image library, two-year licence.

Prints

€180–1,400

Editions of fifteen, 24×30 cm to 100×125 cm, unframed.
