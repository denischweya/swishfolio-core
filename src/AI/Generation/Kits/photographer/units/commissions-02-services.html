B

Sittings for authors, founders, makers and performers, in the Marvila studio or wherever you work. One window, one reflector, ninety unhurried minutes.

from €950 per sitting

Travel & hospitality

Image libraries for small hotels, guesthouses, restaurants and tourism boards. Architecture, rooms, table and landscape, made over a stay rather than a visit.

from €2,400 per day
