Good to know

Three quick answers

How quickly will I hear back?

Within two working days, usually sooner. If I am on location without signal, an auto-reply will say when I am back.

What should a first email include?

Rough dates, the place, who the work is for and where it will appear. A budget range helps but is not required to start the conversation.

Can I visit the studio to see prints?

Yes, Tuesday to Thursday by appointment. The studio is ten minutes on foot from Marvila station.
