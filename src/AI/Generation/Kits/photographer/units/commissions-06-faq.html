Before you write

Anything not covered here, ask. Short emails are welcome.

Do you shoot digital as well as film?

Yes. Roughly two thirds of commissioned work is medium-format and 35mm film; the rest is digital, used when turnaround is under a week or light levels demand it. The two are graded to sit together in one edit.

How far ahead should I book?

Editorial and hospitality work is usually booked eight to twelve weeks out. Portrait sittings in Lisbon can often be arranged within three weeks. The calendar for spring 2027 opens in January.

What does the licence cover?

Editorial commissions include first-use print and web rights for the commissioning title. Hospitality libraries come with a two-year licence for your own channels. Extensions and buy-outs are quoted simply, as a percentage of the original fee.

Do you travel, and who pays for it?

I work throughout Europe and North Africa and travel by train where the schedule allows. Travel and accommodation are billed at cost, agreed in the treatment, with no day-rate charged for travel days under four hours.

Can I buy a print of something on this site?

Almost certainly. Prints are made to order on cotton rag in editions of fifteen, signed and numbered on the reverse. Write with the frame number and a size, and you will get a price and a lead time, usually three weeks.

Do you photograph weddings or events?

No. It is a different craft and there are people who do it far better. I am happy to recommend two or three in Lisbon and Porto.
