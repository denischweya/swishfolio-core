About · Idun Marlowe, b. 1989

I photograph the hour when nothing much is happening.

I grew up between Bergen and the Dorset coast, studied printmaking, and came to photography sideways, as a way of making notes for etchings I never got round to. The notes turned out to be the work.

Since 2017 I have been based in Lisbon, in a studio on the top floor of a former sail loft in Marvila. From there I make editorial stories for magazines and publishers, portraits of people who make things, and image libraries for small hotels that would rather not look like hotels.

Most of it is shot on a thirty-year-old medium-format camera and a 35mm rangefinder, in available light. I work alone or with one assistant, carry very little, and would rather wait for a cloud than fix it afterwards.
