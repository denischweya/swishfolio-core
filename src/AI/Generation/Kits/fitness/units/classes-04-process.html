Your first visit

Nervous? Everyone was once.

  1. 01

    Arrive ten minutes early

    We’ll show you the lockers, showers and where the coffee is. Bring a water bottle and a towel if you like. We have both.

  2. 02

    Meet your coach

    Tell them about injuries, goals or nerves. They’ll set you up with weights, springs or options that suit you.

  3. 03

    Train at your level

    Every class is scaled. Nobody is watching you, except the coach, who is making sure you’re doing it well.

  4. 04

    Stay for the plunge

    Intro members get one free recovery session. Ninety seconds at 4°C is the best way to end a first week.
