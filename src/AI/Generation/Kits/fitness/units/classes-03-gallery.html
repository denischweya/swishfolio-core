What to expect

Every class, explained.

Intensity

Strength

Barbell and kettlebell training on a 12-week written programme. Every session has a main lift, accessory work and a short finisher, and your numbers are logged so the weights go up on purpose.

  • All levels, loads are individual
  • Lifting shoes optional, flat trainers fine
  • Strength 45 · Barbell Club · Kettlebell 30 · Open Barbell

Intensity

HIIT

Intervals on rowers, air bikes, sleds and the floor. Heart-rate zones on screen so you can see the work. Hard, short, and finished with a proper cool-down.

  • Every move has a lower-impact option
  • Bring water. You’ll need it
  • Engine · Burn 40 · Lunch Burn · Friday Burn

Intensity

Reformer

Twelve studio-grade reformers in a light-filled room. Slow, controlled, surprisingly tough work for core, glutes and posture.

  • Start with Reformer Basics if new
  • Grip socks required (we sell them)
  • Reformer Basics · Flow · Core · Express

Intensity

Yoga & mobility

Vinyasa for flow and heat, mobility labs for hips, shoulders and spines that sit all day. A perfect partner to heavy lifting days.

  • Mats and props provided
  • Beginner-friendly, every class
  • Slow Flow · Vinyasa · Desk Reset · Restore

Intensity

Recovery

Coach-led contrast sessions: three rounds of sauna and plunge with guided breathing in between. Calmer than it sounds, and addictive.

  • Unlimited+ or A$30 drop-in
  • Swimwear and a water bottle
  • Heat + Ice · Contrast Club
