Questions

Before you sign up.

Anything else? Message the front desk on (03) 9012 4471. A real person answers.

Can I pause my membership?

Yes, up to eight weeks a year for travel, illness or life. Pauses are free and you can set them in the app.

What if I’m a complete beginner?

Most of our members were. Every class is coached and scaled, and Reformer Basics and Strength 45 are good first picks. Your coach will check in before you start.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. Memberships roll monthly and can be cancelled with 30 days’ notice. Class packs simply expire at the end of their validity.

Do you have showers and lockers?

Five private showers, a vanity area with hairdryers, and free day lockers. Unlimited+ members also get towel service.

Can I share a class pack?

Yes, class packs can be shared with one nominated person, which makes them popular with couples and training partners.
