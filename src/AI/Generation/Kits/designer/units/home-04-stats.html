03 · How I work

Strategy first. Then the beautiful part.

Every project starts with two weeks of listening: your team, your customers, the market you're actually in rather than the one in the deck. By the end of it we agree on what the brand has to achieve, in writing.

Only then do I design, in fewer, sharper directions rather than a wall of options. You get one owner, one opinion and work that still looks right in five years, which is the only test that matters.
