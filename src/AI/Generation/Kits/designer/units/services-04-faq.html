Questions

Before we start.

Anything else? Ask on the first call. It is free, thirty minutes and comes with no pitch deck.

How long does a brand identity take?

Most identities take ten to twelve weeks from the first workshop to final files. A focused Identity Sprint takes five. Packaging and product work add time depending on print and engineering schedules.

Do you work with clients outside New Zealand?

Yes. About half my clients are in Australia, the UK and the US. Workshops happen over video, and I travel for the important moments, like press checks and shoots, when it matters.

Will we own everything you design?

Yes. On final payment you own the identity, artwork and source files outright. Typefaces are licensed in your name, and I hand over a tidy, documented asset library.

Can you work with our in-house team or agency?

Often the best projects do. I am happy to set the system and then support your team or developers, with review sessions and a shared Figma library.

How do payments work?

Fixed fees, agreed in a written proposal. Usually 40% to begin, 30% at the creative presentation and 30% on delivery. Retainers are billed monthly in advance.
