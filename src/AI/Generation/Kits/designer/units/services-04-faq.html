Practical questions

How quickly can you start?

Usually six to eight weeks out. Sprints can sometimes slot in sooner. Ask, and I'll tell you honestly rather than squeeze you in badly.

Do you work with early-stage companies?

Often. A sprint is usually the right size at that stage, enough to get you looking credible in front of customers and investors without spending the round on it.

Who builds the website or the packaging afterwards?

Your team, or developers and producers I've worked with for years and can introduce you to. I stay close through production either way. That's where identities usually go wrong.

How do payments work?

Fixed price, agreed up front, in three instalments. No hourly billing and no surprise change requests, if the scope genuinely moves, we agree it in writing first.
