Questions

The practical bits.

How far ahead do you book?

Usually six to ten weeks. I am currently booking projects that start in February 2027, with the odd sprint slot sooner.

Is there a minimum budget?

Most projects start from NZ$14k. If your budget is smaller, ask anyway: I can often suggest a sensible first step or a designer who fits.

Can we visit the studio?

Gladly, if you are in Wellington. The studio is above a bookshop on Cuba Street, and the coffee is good.
