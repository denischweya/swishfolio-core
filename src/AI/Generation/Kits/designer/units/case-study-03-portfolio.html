The challenge

Aulde had a loyal following, an excellent product and packaging that had grown one label at a time. Nine products used four typefaces. The founder’s hand-drawn leaf appeared in three versions. Buyers liked the formulas but told her the range “wasn’t ready for the floor”.

The brief: make Aulde look as considered as the products inside, keep the customers who already loved it, and be ready for national retail in four months.

  • 1thing to keep: the amber glass
