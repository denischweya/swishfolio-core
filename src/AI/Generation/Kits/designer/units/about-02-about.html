I fell for design the first time a typeface changed how a sentence felt.

That was a student project in 2012, and I have been chasing the feeling since: the moment a brand stops looking designed and starts looking inevitable.

Studio life in Sydney taught me packaging, production and how to survive a press check at 2am. Berlin taught me restraint, and that a good grid is a kindness. In 2021 I came home to Wellington and opened a studio of one, so I could work closely with the people who actually run the businesses I design for.

Today I take on four projects a quarter. Each is designed by me, with a small circle of photographers, developers and packaging engineers I trust completely.

  1. 2015
    Junior designer, Sydney

    Packaging and identity for food and drink brands at a twelve-person studio.

  2. 2018
    Senior designer, Berlin

    Led identity projects for galleries, publishers and two start-ups at a type-led studio.

  3. 2021
    Went independent

    Moved home to Wellington and opened a one-person studio on Cuba Street.

  4. 2024
    Teaching & talks

    Guest tutor in typography and a regular speaker on design for small brands.

  5. 2026
    60+ brands

    Identities, ranges and products launched across four continents.
