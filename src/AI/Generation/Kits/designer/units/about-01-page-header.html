About

Eleven years of making things look like what they are.

I'm Mara Lindqvist, a designer in Stockholm. I work on identity, packaging and digital products, usually all three, because they're the same problem wearing different clothes.

I trained at Konstfack, spent four years at a Stockholm agency learning how brands are built and two more as design lead at a fintech learning how they're maintained. Since 2021 I've run my own studio: just me, a small bench of collaborators I trust, and a deliberately short client list.

The work I'm proudest of is rarely the loudest. It's the packaging that still sells four years later, the design system that didn't need rebuilding, the app people describe as calm without knowing why. I care a great deal about craft and not at all about trends.

Outside the studio I print posters on a very temperamental risograph, teach one term a year at Konstfack, and spend as much of the summer as possible on the archipelago.
