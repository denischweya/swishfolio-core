The process

We started on the shop floor, not in a sketchbook: two weeks of store visits, customer interviews at the markets and a shelf audit of every competitor Aulde would sit beside.

  1. Research

    Store visits, eleven customer interviews and a shelf audit. The insight: customers trusted the amber glass more than the logo.

  2. Concept

    Three routes presented on real bottles. The winner, “Apothecary”, treats each product like a carefully labelled remedy.

  3. System

    A custom serif wordmark, one hero ingredient set large on every label, and a stone, sand and rust palette.

  4. Rollout

    Dielines, press checks, counter units and launch photography, all art directed in one ten-day push.
