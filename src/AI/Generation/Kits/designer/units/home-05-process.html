How a project runs

Ten weeks, four steps, no surprises.

A clear rhythm so you always know what is happening and what comes next. You talk to me from the first call to the final file.

  1. 01

    Week 1 to 2

    Listen

    Workshops, interviews and a hard look at the market. We agree the problem before anyone opens a sketchbook.

  2. 02

    Week 2 to 4

    Define

    A short written strategy and two or three distinct creative routes, presented in person and in context.

  3. 03

    Week 4 to 9

    Design

    One route, refined into a full system and tested on the real things: packs, screens, signs and stationery.

  4. 04

    Week 9 onward

    Deliver

    Guidelines, files and a launch kit, plus press checks and handover sessions with your team.
