Questions

Before you book.

Still unsure? Call (303) 555-0148 and ask for a comfort advisor. They don’t work on commission.

Do you service every brand?

Yes. We repair and maintain every major furnace, boiler, AC, heat pump and mini-split brand. For new systems we install three manufacturers we trust and will explain why we recommend one over another for your house.

What does the $89 diagnostic cover?

A full diagnosis of one system by a NATE-certified technician, a written explanation of what we found and a flat-rate price for the fix. If you go ahead with the repair, the $89 is taken off. Comfort Club members never pay it.

How fast can you replace a system?

Most replacements are installed within two to four days of your quote, usually in a single day on site. In an emergency, we can often install the next day and lend you heaters until then.

Do you pull permits?

Always, for every installation that needs one. We schedule the city inspection and attend it, so you don’t have to take time off.

What warranty do I get?

Two years on labour for every repair. New systems get the manufacturer’s 10-year parts warranty plus our own 10-year labour warranty when registered, which we do for you.
