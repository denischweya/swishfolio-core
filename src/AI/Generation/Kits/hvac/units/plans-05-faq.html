Questions

Plan questions.

Still unsure? Call (303) 555-0148 and ask for a comfort advisor. They don’t work on commission.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, with no fee. Monthly plans stop at the end of the month; yearly plans are refunded for unused months, minus any discounts you have already used.

When are tune-ups scheduled?

We contact you to book cooling tune-ups from March to May and heating tune-ups from August to October, before the busy seasons start. You choose the day and window.

Does the plan cover parts?

Plans cover the tune-ups, discounts and priority. Parts and repairs are charged at member prices. Total Home members get 10-year parts and labour on new systems we install.

I have two furnaces. Do I need two plans?

Each additional system is $10 a month on any plan, so a house with two furnaces and two ACs on Comfort is $39 a month.

Do I have to have bought my system from you?

No. We look after systems from any installer and any brand, as long as it passes the first-visit safety check.
