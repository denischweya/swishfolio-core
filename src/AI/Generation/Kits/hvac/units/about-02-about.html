Ray Castellanos spent fifteen years working for other heating companies before he decided there was a better way to treat customers, and technicians.

He and Linda started Northwind in 2004 with one van and a simple promise: show up in the window you said, tell people the price before you start, and never sell someone a system they don’t need. Twenty-two years later, it’s still the whole business plan.

Every Northwind technician is a full-time employee with benefits, paid training and no sales commission. That last part matters: it means the person in your basement has no reason to recommend anything other than what is right.

  1. 2004

    One van, one phone

    Ray and Linda Castellanos start Northwind from their garage in Globeville. Ray answers the emergency line himself for three years.

  2. 2011

    First apprentices

    The paid apprenticeship begins. Nine of today’s lead technicians started there.

  3. 2017

    Commercial team

    A dedicated crew for restaurants, practices and offices, with after-hours installs.

  4. 2021

    Heat pumps go mainstream

    Cold-climate heat pumps pass furnaces as our most-installed heating system.

  5. 2026

    38 technicians, 52 vans

    Still family-owned, now from a training centre and warehouse in RiNo.
