Before we arrive

Three things that make the visit faster.

None of these are required, but each one saves a few minutes and sometimes a second trip.

  1. 1

    Photo of the label

    Text us a picture of the sticker on your furnace or outdoor unit. We’ll bring the right parts.

  2. 2

    Clear a path

    A clear way to the furnace, water heater and outdoor unit, and pets settled somewhere comfortable.

  3. 3

    Note what you noticed

    Noises, smells, error codes, when it started. Small details often point straight to the fault.
