What a tune-up includes

Twenty-one checks, with photos, every visit.

A tune-up is not a filter swap and a sticker. Our technicians work through the same checklist every time and send you a written report with readings and photos.

Autumn · heating

  1. Test for carbon monoxide at the flue and in living spaces
  2. Inspect heat exchanger for cracks with a camera
  3. Clean and test flame sensor and igniter
  4. Measure gas pressure and temperature rise
  5. Check inducer, blower motor and capacitor
  6. Test safety limits and flame rollout switches
  7. Tighten electrical connections
  8. Check and replace the filter
  9. Calibrate the thermostat
  10. Clear the condensate line and trap

Spring · cooling

  1. Wash the outdoor coil
  2. Measure refrigerant charge (superheat / subcool)
  3. Test capacitor and contactor
  4. Check fan motor amps and blade
  5. Clean evaporator coil where accessible
  6. Flush and treat the condensate drain
  7. Measure airflow and temperature split
  8. Inspect line-set insulation
  9. Level and secure the outdoor unit
  10. Clear plants and debris to 2 ft
  11. Written report with photos
