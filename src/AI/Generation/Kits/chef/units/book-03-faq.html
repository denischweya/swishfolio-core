Booking questions

The practical bits.

How much notice do you need?

For Saturdays, two to three months is safest. Weeknights and lunches can often be arranged within a fortnight. Weddings and villa weeks usually book six to twelve months ahead.

What happens to my deposit if I cancel?

More than thirty days before, it is refunded in full. Within thirty days it can move to another date in the following six months.

Do you travel outside London?

Yes. The Home Counties for a flat £60, and Portugal, Mallorca, Provence and Tuscany for villa weeks and celebrations, with travel quoted at cost.

Are you insured and food-hygiene certified?

Yes, Level 3 food hygiene, public liability insurance, and a written allergen process for every menu.
