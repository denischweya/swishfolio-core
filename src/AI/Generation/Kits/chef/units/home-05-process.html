The evening

You host. I handle everything else.

A typical dinner party, from the moment I ring your doorbell to the moment I quietly let myself out.

  1. 17:00

    I arrive

    With the shopping, my knives and one or two of my team. Your kitchen becomes mine for the evening.

  2. 19:30

    Guests arrive

    Canapés and a first drink are already going round. The table is laid; you have nothing to do.

  3. 20:15

    Dinner

    Courses come out on a gentle rhythm. I introduce each one at the table, briefly.

  4. 22:30

    Sweet things

    Dessert, coffee and something warm from the oven while you stay seated.

  5. 23:30

    We leave

    Everything washed, dried and put away. Leftovers labelled in the fridge for tomorrow.
