Menu questions

Before you choose.

Anything else, ask on the enquiry form. I read every one myself.

Can I mix dishes from different menus?

Yes. Most final menus borrow from all three. We agree yours on a call, then I send a written version for you to approve.

Do you bring everything?

I bring the food, my own knives, pans and any specialist equipment. You need a working oven, a hob and somewhere to put twenty plates. I’ll ask about your kitchen before the evening.

How far ahead is the menu fixed?

Seven days before, when final numbers and dietary needs are confirmed. Fish and vegetables are chosen at the market on the day.

Is service included?

For dinner parties and tasting menus, yes, one member of my team for every eight guests. Larger events are quoted with a full team.
