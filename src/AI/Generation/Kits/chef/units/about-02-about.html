I grew up above my aunt’s tasca in Alfama, where dinner was never served. It was shared.

Ten years in London restaurants taught me precision: how to plate a hundred identical courses, how to run a pass, how to source properly. But the best meals I cooked were always the ones for friends, at home, with no pass between me and the people eating.

Meza (after mesa, Portuguese for “table”) started with a friend’s engagement dinner in 2019. Today it is me and a small team of chefs and waiting staff I trust completely, cooking about two hundred evenings a year.

  1. 2009
    Lisbon

    Trained at culinary school in Lisbon, weekends in my aunt’s tasca in Alfama.

  2. 2012
    London

    Moved to London. Seven years in restaurant kitchens, from commis to sous chef.

  3. 2017
    Head chef

    Ran the kitchen of a forty-cover Soho restaurant for three years.

  4. 2019
    Meza

    Cooked my first private dinner, a friend’s engagement, and never went back.

  5. 2026
    400 tables

    Over four hundred dinners, thirty villa weeks and eleven weddings so far.
