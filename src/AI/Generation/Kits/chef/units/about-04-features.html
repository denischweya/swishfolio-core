House rules

Four things I promise every host.

i.

You won’t lift a finger

From the first canapé to the last pan, my team does everything, including the bins.

ii.

Your kitchen, cleaner

We leave it cleaner than we found it. Hosts mention this more than the food.

iii.

A fixed price

One written quote, agreed in advance. No surprises on the final bill.

iv.

Me, in person

I cook every dinner I take on myself. I don’t send someone in my place.
