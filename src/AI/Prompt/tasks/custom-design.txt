Fonts you may use (exact names; each is self-hosted and only these exist):
{{fonts}}

Palette roles (hex #rrggbb each):
- background: the page background.
- surface: alternate band and panel background, a clear step from background.
- text: body and heading text (7:1 or better on background).
- muted: secondary text (4.5:1 or better on background and surface).
- accent: the main brand colour, used for buttons, links and highlighted words (4.5:1 or better on background).
- accent2: a second colour for small highlights and decoration (3:1 or better on background).
- soft: a gentle tint for filled cards and decorative shapes (text must read on it).
- inverse: background of the bold contrasting bands; the page background colour is the text on it (4.5:1 or better).

Choose:
- fonts.display for headings, fonts.body for running text (never a monospace), fonts.label for small labels, buttons and kickers (may equal body, or be a mono for a technical feel).
- headingWeight 100 to 900 (within the display font's weights), headingCase, headingTracking, headingScale (0.8 restrained to 1.4 huge).
- labelStyle, radius (none for hard edges, pill for very round), borderWidth 0 to 4 px, shadow (hard = offset solid shadows), buttons, links, cards, sections (alternate bands, bordered rules between bands, or flat), spacing, imageStyle, imageFrame, decor (the one decorative motif the site uses, or none).
- name: one or two words. summary: one sentence for the owner about the look, plain words, no dashes.
